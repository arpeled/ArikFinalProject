One significant factor contributing to this issue is the class imbalance in the dataset. The model might be biased towards the majority class, leading to poor performance on the minority classes. Adjustments made to the alpha parameter in FocalLoss and the addition of class weights have not been sufficient to address this.

The AUC-ROC is decent for some classes, but there is a wide variance among classes, and it is poor for others such as Pneumonia and Infiltration. The AUC-ROC measures the ability of the model to distinguish between the classes, but it does not necessarily translate to good performance in terms of precision and recall, especially in imbalanced datasets.

Comparing with previous iterations, it seems that tweaking the dropout rate, learning rate, and the number of epochs did not have a consistent positive impact on the model's performance. The F1-score fluctuated but remained low overall. The best F1-score was achieved in iteration 4, but it still was only 0.1076.
