## ANALYSIS:

The critical issue faced by the model is its tendency to predict almost all samples as negative. This is primarily due to the severe class imbalance, which causes the model to be biased towards the majority class (negative class in this case). This results in very low recall (0.0853), precision (0.1709) and F1-score (0.0246) as the model fails to correctly identify positive cases. 

The model is overfitting, as shown by the validation loss (0.0040) being significantly higher than the training loss (0.0018). This indicates that the model has learned the training data too well and is not generalizing well to new, unseen data. Overfitting is further confirmed by the fact that the model's performance has worsened compared to previous iterations.

The threshold of 0.5 is not suitable for such an imbalanced dataset, which explains why the model is predicting almost all samples as negative. Implementing per-class threshold optimization may help handle class imbalance and improve recall.

In terms of the loss function configuration, the model uses FocalLoss with gamma set to 3.0 and alpha set to 0.7. Considering the results, the model is too focused on hard examples and not enough on the positive class. The gamma parameter may be too high, causing the model to focus excessively on hard, misclassified examples. Furthermore, the alpha parameter, which is supposed to control the weight of the minority class, may not be effective enough.

Comparing this iteration with previous ones, the model's performance has deteriorated. The F1-score and recall have decreased, while the AUC has also slightly decreased. This shows that the model's ability to correctly identify positive cases has worsened.
