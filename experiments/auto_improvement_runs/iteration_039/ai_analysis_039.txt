Focal Loss is designed to address class imbalance by down-weighting easy examples and focusing training on hard negatives. However, in our case, it seems like the model is focusing too much on negatives (easy examples) and not enough on positives (hard examples). 

Comparing this iteration with the previous ones, we can see that F1-score has improved since the introduction of oversampling and dynamic class weights, but it's still not at an acceptable level.
