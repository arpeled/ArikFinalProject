## ANALYSIS

The current model's performance is not satisfactory with F1-Score, Recall, and Precision at extremely low values, despite a decent AUC. Here's a detailed analysis:

1. **Issue of Almost All Negative Predictions**: The model is predicting almost all samples as negative. This is likely due to the highly imbalanced nature of the data. The model seems to be biased towards the majority class (negative class) to minimize loss, which contributes to the high AUC but at the cost of poor F1-Score, Recall, and Precision.

2. **Poor Recall and F1-Score**: The extremely low recall indicates that the model is not able to correctly identify positive samples, which is a major concern in medical diagnoses where false negatives can have serious implications. Moreover, the low F1-Score suggests that the model is not balancing precision and recall effectively.

3. **Comparison with Previous Iterations**: Iteration 16 seems to be the most successful so far, with the highest F1 score of 0.0526. It's worth noting that Iteration 16 used a lower learning rate, a higher dropout rate, and a higher number of epochs, as well as optimal threshold optimization based on F1. However, the model overfit during Iteration 16.

4. **Overfitting**: The model is overfitting, as seen from the higher validation loss compared to the training loss. This indicates that the model is not generalizing well to unseen data and is memorizing the training data instead.

5. **Loss Function Configuration**: The model uses a focal loss function with a gamma of 3.0 and class weights. Although this configuration is generally effective for imbalanced datasets, the gamma value might be too high, which could be putting too much focus on hard, misclassified examples and causing the model to miss out on easier, correctly classified examples.
